Study Buddy

Privacy Policy

Last updated 11 November 2026

Study Buddy is a focus app: you study, and your time grows a small home for Buddy. Everything you do in the app is kept on your iPhone or iPad. There are no accounts and no servers of our own.

The short version: Your subjects, study sessions, progress, home and photos stay on your device. The app sends usage statistics and masked screen recordings that are not linked to your identity to Amplitude (you can switch this off in Settings), and anonymous subscription statistics to RevenueCat so we can run Study Buddy Pro. Ideas and messages you choose to send us go through WishKit. No ads, no tracking across apps, no selling of data.

Who we are

Study Buddy is developed by Alfa Edge AS (organisation number 928 634 671, Oslo, Norway), the data controller for the limited data described here. Questions about this policy or your privacy: sander.b.lyngstad@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

The app stores the following only on your device, in the app's own storage:

  • your subjects, study sessions and their length, Credits, XP, level and weekly goal;
  • the furniture you own and how your home is arranged, and the name you give Buddy;
  • your settings (music, notifications, usage data choice).

We cannot see or access any of this. It is included in your own device or iCloud backups like other app data, and it is deleted when you delete the app.

Usage statistics (you can switch them off)

By using the app you accept that it sends usage statistics. You can switch this off at any time in Settings → Privacy → Share usage data; nothing more is sent after that. The app sends these events to Amplitude, our analytics provider:

  • a study session started or finished (planned and focused minutes, whether it was completed, Credits and XP earned, your level);
  • furniture bought with in-app Credits (which item and its price in Credits), and a room unlocked;
  • a level reached, and a seasonal item earned with study time;
  • the Study Buddy Pro page shown and closed (where it was opened from and for how long), and Pro purchases, trials, restores and subscription changes (which plan, the price and currency, whether it was a free trial);
  • which onboarding pages were seen and how long onboarding took;
  • whether you allowed notifications or Focus Mode, a notification opened, a Live Activity started or ended, a Study Capture photo saved (never the photo or which apps you chose), and a share card shown or shared;
  • when the app was opened and closed (app sessions).

Session recordings. While usage statistics are on, the app also sends Amplitude a recording of how you move through the screens (taps, scrolling and which screens you open), so we can see where the app is hard to use. All text in the recording is hidden, including what you type, your subject names and Buddy's name, and the camera and Study Capture photos are left out entirely. The recordings are tied only to the same random identifier, never to your name or an account, are not used for advertising, and stop as soon as you switch usage data off.

Amplitude also keeps a short summary for this random identifier: your level, Credits and total study minutes, whether you have Pro and which plan, whether onboarding is done, and the app's build number and whether it came from the App Store or TestFlight.

With these events Amplitude receives technical information such as the device model, iOS version, app version and language, and a random identifier that the app creates for Amplitude (a new one if you reinstall the app; it is not the advertising identifier or Apple's identifier for vendors). We have turned off collection of IP address, country, city, region, DMA and mobile carrier, so Amplitude receives no location. This data is pseudonymous: it is not linked to your name, email or any account (the app has none), is not used for advertising, and is not combined with data from other apps or websites. It is processed on Amplitude's servers in the United States, under Amplitude's data processing terms and the safeguards they provide for transfers from the EU/EEA.

The usage statistics are kept for as long as needed to improve the app, and no longer than Amplitude's retention setting for our project.

The legal basis is our legitimate interest in improving the app (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)). You can object at any time by switching usage data off in Settings.

Subscription statistics (RevenueCat)

To understand how Study Buddy Pro is doing (for example how many trials turn into subscriptions, renewals and cancellations), the app uses RevenueCat. It only watches the purchases you make through Apple; it does not handle payment, and Apple still decides whether Pro is active. Because it is part of how we run the subscription, it is not switched off by Share usage data.

RevenueCat receives your App Store purchase history for Study Buddy (which plan, the price and currency, the purchase, trial, renewal and cancellation dates, and Apple's transaction identifiers), the store country and language, the app and iOS version and the device model, and a random identifier that RevenueCat creates for this install. Like any internet service it sees your IP address when the app connects. We do not give RevenueCat your name, email or any other contact information, and do not use it for advertising. RevenueCat processes this data on our behalf on servers in the United States, under its data processing terms and the safeguards it provides for transfers from the EU/EEA. The data is kept while our RevenueCat project exists; we can delete it on request.

The legal basis is our legitimate interest in understanding and running the subscription (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)). The data is not linked to your identity. You can object or ask for deletion by emailing us.

Ideas, votes and chat (WishKit)

In Settings → Feedback you can suggest ideas, vote on them and chat with the developer. This is provided by WishKit, and only what you choose to write or vote is sent. WishKit receives your ideas, votes and messages, a random identifier that WishKit creates on your device so your votes and chat stay together, and the app's name and identifier. It does not receive your name or email from the app.

New ideas are only shown to other users after we have approved them. Please don't write personal information in ideas or messages. WishKit stores this on servers in the United States (Heroku on Amazon Web Services) and keeps it until we delete it; ask us and we will delete your ideas and messages. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in hearing from users and answering them (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).

If you send a problem report

If something goes wrong, the app can prepare an email to us with a short report. It is only sent if you choose to send it from your own email app, and you can read and change it first. It contains what you write, the app version, the device model, the iOS version, the time, a short description of the error and a report number, and your email address comes with the email. We use it only to fix the problem, and we delete it when it is no longer needed or when you ask.

Permissions, and why the app asks

  • Camera — only when you tap Capture during a study session, to photograph notes, slides or the board.
  • Photos (add only) — to save those photos to your own Photos library. The app cannot read your library, and photos are never uploaded.
  • Notifications — a local notification when a timed session is done, and, if you start a free trial, a reminder the day before it ends. They are created on your device; no notification server is used. Study Buddy never sends reminders to study.
  • Screen Time (Focus Mode) — only if you turn Focus Mode on, to block the apps you choose while you study. Your app selection stays on your device and is handled by Apple's Screen Time; we never see it.

You can change any of these in Settings → Study Buddy on your iPhone or iPad. Studying works without them.

Purchases

Study Buddy Pro is sold as an auto-renewing subscription through Apple's App Store. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; we never receive your payment details. The app only learns from Apple whether Pro is active. Furniture bought with in-app Credits involves no money. Subscription purchases are also reported to RevenueCat and, unless usage data is switched off, to Amplitude, as described above.

Sharing

When you choose to share a card (for example after a session), the app creates the picture on your device and hands it to the iOS share sheet. What happens next is up to you and the app you share to.

Children

Study Buddy is made for students. It has no accounts, no ads and no way for users to message each other; ideas only appear for others after we approve them. The data that can leave the device is the usage statistics (until switched off) and the anonymous subscription statistics described above, and ideas, messages or problem reports a user chooses to send. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Testing with TestFlight

If you test Study Buddy before release through Apple's TestFlight, Apple shares your email address (or name) with us, and the crash reports, screenshots and feedback you choose to send from TestFlight.

Security

Everything the app sends is sent encrypted (HTTPS), and only to the services named here. Data on your device is protected by iOS and iPadOS like other app data. No system is perfectly secure, but we collect as little as possible so that there is little to protect.

Your rights

You can switch usage statistics off at any time in Settings. Because these statistics are not linked to your identity, we usually cannot single out your data, but you are welcome to contact us with any request (access, deletion, objection) and we will help as far as we can. You also have the right to complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority (Datatilsynet) or your local authority.

California residents: we do not sell or share personal information as defined by the CCPA/CPRA, and do not use it for cross-context behavioural advertising. You may ask what we hold about you and ask us to delete it, and we will not treat you differently for asking.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects privacy, we will update this page and the date at the top before that change ships, and mention important changes in the app's update notes.
