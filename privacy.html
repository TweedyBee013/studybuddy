Study Buddy

Privacy Policy

Last updated 31 October 2026

Study Buddy is a focus app: you study, and your time grows a small home for Buddy. Everything you do in the app is kept on your iPhone. There are no accounts and no servers of our own.

The short version: Your subjects, study sessions, progress, home and photos stay on your device. If you say yes when the app asks, it sends anonymous usage statistics to our analytics provider, Amplitude, to help us improve the app. You can say no, or switch it off at any time in Settings. No ads, no tracking across apps, no selling of data.

Who we are

Study Buddy is developed by Alfa Edge AS (Norway), the data controller for the limited data described here. Questions about this policy or your privacy: sander.b.lyngstad@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

The app stores the following only on your iPhone, in the app's own storage:

  • your subjects, study sessions and their length, Credits, XP, level and weekly goal;
  • the furniture you own and how your home is arranged, and the name you give Buddy;
  • your settings (music, notifications, usage data choice).

We cannot see or access any of this. It is included in your own iPhone or iCloud backups like other app data, and it is deleted when you delete the app.

Anonymous usage statistics (only with your consent)

During setup the app asks whether you want to share anonymous usage data. Nothing is sent before you answer yes, and you can change your answer at any time in Settings → Privacy → Share anonymous usage data. If you say yes, the app sends these events to Amplitude, our analytics provider:

  • a study session started or finished (planned and focused minutes, whether it was completed, Credits and XP earned, your level);
  • furniture bought with in-app Credits (which item and its price in Credits), and a room unlocked;
  • a share card shown or shared;
  • when the app was opened and closed (app sessions).

With these events Amplitude receives technical information such as the device model, iOS version, app version and language, and a device identifier that Apple provides per app developer (not the advertising identifier). We have turned off collection of IP address, city, region and mobile carrier. The data is not linked to your name, email or any account (the app has none), is not used for advertising, and is not combined with data from other apps or websites. It is processed on Amplitude's servers in the United States, under Amplitude's data processing terms and the safeguards they provide for transfers from the EU/EEA.

The legal basis is your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time in Settings.

Permissions, and why the app asks

  • Camera — only when you tap Capture during a study session, to photograph notes, slides or the board.
  • Photos (add only) — to save those photos to your own Photos library. The app cannot read your library, and photos are never uploaded.
  • Notifications — a local notification when a timed session is done, and, if you start a free trial, a reminder the day before it ends. They are created on your iPhone; no notification server is used. Study Buddy never sends reminders to study.
  • Screen Time (Focus Mode) — only if you turn Focus Mode on, to block the apps you choose while you study. Your app selection stays on your device and is handled by Apple's Screen Time; we never see it.

You can change any of these in Settings → Study Buddy on your iPhone. Studying works without them.

Purchases

Study Buddy Pro is sold as an auto-renewing subscription through Apple's App Store. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; we never receive your payment details. The app only learns from Apple whether Pro is active. Furniture bought with in-app Credits involves no money.

Sharing

When you choose to share a card (for example after a session), the app creates the picture on your device and hands it to the iOS share sheet. What happens next is up to you and the app you share to.

Children

Study Buddy has no accounts, no ads, no chat and no way for users to contact each other. The only data that can leave the device is the anonymous usage statistics described above, and only after a yes.

Your rights

You can withdraw consent to usage statistics at any time in Settings. Because these statistics are not linked to your identity, we usually cannot single out your data, but you are welcome to contact us with any request (access, deletion, objection) and we will help as far as we can. You also have the right to complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority (Datatilsynet) or your local authority.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects privacy, we will update this page and the date at the top before that change ships.
