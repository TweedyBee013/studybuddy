Common questions

  • Where is my progress stored? On your iPhone. There is no account; deleting the app deletes your progress.
  • Can I study without Focus Mode, Capture or notifications? Yes. They are optional; studying always works.
  • How do I manage or cancel Study Buddy Pro? In iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Purchases can be restored from the Pro page in the app.
  • How do I stop sharing usage data? In the app: Settings → Privacy → Share anonymous usage data.
